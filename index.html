Hey there! 👋 I'm Mihir, a software engineer and AI researcher at General Motors.

Lately, I have been interested in the intersection of AI interpretability and Formal Methods, particularly in developing methods to ensure the safety and reliability of AI systems.

In order to develop a reliable way of interacting with Agents I think it is necessary to first develop methods to interpret their behavior and then I believe developing formal verification techniques will be crucial.

I am fascinated by the fact that neural networks learn to represent complex patterns in the form of circuits. I believe studying this will have deeper implications on how we design and think about Large Language Models in the future.
